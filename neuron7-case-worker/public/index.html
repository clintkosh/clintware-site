Implementation Readiness Gate

Prevent the same miss before the next date becomes committed.

Automation: an unknown/custom connector in CRM triggers Solution/Engineering review and blocks the implementation date from being treated as customer-committed until the exception is approved.

What this prevents

Timeline commitments before data sources, connector readiness, access/security, customer dependencies, content quality and ROI measurement are understood.

What it does not do

It does not turn CS into Engineering project management. It creates a shared decision boundary, then lets each team own its work.

Acceptable Use and Likeness Policy

Clintware is designed for ambitious custom requests. Most ideas can be fulfilled as written or translated into an original alternative. Clintware reviews each request before production and may decline, narrow, or modify details when consent, safety, technical quality, law, platform rules, or third-party rights require it.

Required consent

  • Upload only images you own or are authorized to use.
  • Obtain parent or legal-guardian permission for identifiable minors.
  • Do not use another person’s likeness to deceive, harass, defraud, exploit, or imply endorsement.

Not accepted

  • Sexual or intimate imagery, including synthetic intimate content.
  • Exploitative or sexualized depictions of minors or young-looking persons.
  • Unauthorized or deceptive use of a real person’s likeness, including false endorsement or fabricated events presented as authentic.
  • Harassment, hate, threats, graphic violence, criminal facilitation, or fraud.
  • Official logos, mascots, uniforms, copyrighted characters, or branded scenes unless rights are documented and separately approved.
  • Government IDs, financial documents, medical records, or other unnecessarily sensitive files.

Original alternatives

You may describe the inspiration, mood, era, genre, city, occupation, wardrobe, or type of event you want. Clintware may translate restricted details into original ones, such as maroon-and-white college spirit instead of an official university package, an original stadium performer instead of a named celebrity, an art-deco Manhattan tower instead of branding a private landmark, or a custom city guardian instead of a protected franchise character.

Public places and broad themes

Requests may reference real cities, public landscapes, historical periods, broad artistic media, occupations, and genres. Clintware does not promise access to restricted interiors, exact private-property branding, a living artist’s signature style, official uniforms, or false affiliation with any person or organization.

Removal requests

To report an unauthorized likeness or request removal of material controlled by Clintware, email studio@clintware.com with the relevant order reference, your relationship to the depicted person, and enough information to verify the request.

Service Terms

Clintware Family Media Studio provides manually reviewed, AI-assisted custom digital artwork. Submitting a request does not guarantee acceptance. Clintware may decline or translate restricted details into an original alternative.

Orders and PayPal payment

Free previews require no payment. Approved paid orders are billed through an itemized PayPal invoice unless a PayPal-hosted Payment Link is shown. Production begins only after the required payment is confirmed. The customer must use an accurate email address that can receive PayPal notices and order communications.

Customer responsibilities

You must own or have permission to use every uploaded file and every depicted person’s likeness. Guardian permission is required for identifiable minors. You must not submit deceptive, exploitative, intimate, unlawful, infringing, or unauthorized material.

Deliverables and licenses

The selected package controls the number of images, revision rounds, file formats, and personal-use or commercial-use license. No exclusive federal copyright is promised for output that may not qualify for copyright protection.

Timing and revisions

Delivery timing is confirmed after review and payment. Requests beyond the included revision rounds may require an additional PayPal invoice.

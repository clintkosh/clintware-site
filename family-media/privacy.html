Privacy Policy

Clintware collects the information needed to review and fulfill an order: name, email, selected package, request description, consent confirmations, uploaded source images, payment status, correspondence, and delivered files.

PayPal

PayPal processes paid transactions under its own privacy terms. Clintware does not receive or store the customer’s full card number or PayPal password. Clintware may receive transaction identifiers, payer information, invoice status, and dispute information needed to reconcile the order.

Storage and deletion

The included PHP handler stores source uploads in a private folder outside the public web root. The launch target is deletion within 30 days after final delivery unless earlier deletion is requested or longer retention is authorized.

Use and sharing

Customer files are used for review, production, delivery, support, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. Customer work is not used publicly without a separate written release.

Requests

Email studio@clintware.com for access, correction, deletion, or an unauthorized-likeness report.

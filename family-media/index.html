Simple fulfillment

How orders work

1. Submit

Choose a package, upload authorized photos, describe the scene, and accept the likeness and usage terms.

2. Review and invoice

Clintware reviews the request. Approved paid orders receive a PayPal invoice unless a direct PayPal link is configured.

3. Produce and deliver

Production starts after payment. A proof is emailed for approval, then final files and the license are delivered.

Clear boundaries

What the studio will and will not create

Allowed

  • Customers and family members with permission
  • Original fantasy, studio, travel, and seasonal scenes
  • Generic school-spirit colors without protected marks
  • Pets, coloring pages, calendars, invitations, and portraits

Declined or licensed separately

  • Celebrity or public-figure impersonation
  • Official university, team, movie, or character branding
  • Deceptive, intimate, exploitative, or illegal content
  • Photos uploaded without permission or guardian consent
Questions

Before customers order

How does PayPal payment work?

By default, Clintware reviews the request and sends an itemized PayPal invoice to the customer email. Production begins after the paid status is verified. Optional PayPal-hosted Payment Links can replace the invoice-request buttons.

Do customers own the copyright?

Customers receive the license stated in their package. AI-assisted outputs may not qualify for exclusive copyright unless sufficient human-authored creative expression is present.

How long are uploaded photos kept?

The launch policy is to delete uploaded source photos and working files within 30 days after final delivery, unless the customer requests earlier deletion or authorizes longer retention.

Method

What this sample is demonstrating

  • Latest sent report defines the exact interval; drafts do not move the baseline.
  • Application actions and deduped unique company-role applications are separate numbers.
  • Network/search-firm intros stay outside employer interview totals until tied to a specific role.
  • Official market data should lead; platform/industry evidence adds detail; Reddit/community evidence is qualitative only.
  • Full market sections keep data tables and visual comparisons rather than collapsing into paragraphs.
  • A replacement report must preserve still-valid sections/charts from approved earlier versions.
  • All people, companies, roles, counts, and process events in this sample are fictional.

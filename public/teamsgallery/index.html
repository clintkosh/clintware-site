The composition rule

Build for the webcam first.

A beautiful office photo can still make a terrible virtual background if the desk, windows, or skyline collide with the person on camera.

1. Keep the foreground open.

No giant desk edge, monitor, coffee cup, or decorative object covering the caller.

2. Center the empty chair.

The chair gives the caller a believable place to occupy without putting another visual subject behind them.

3. Put the city around the face.

Landmarks and bright skyline features stay toward the outer thirds so the caller remains the focal point.

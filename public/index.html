Core thesis

Local when control matters. AI where it creates leverage.

Clintware is building toward reusable infrastructure that can support standalone products, self-hosted deployments, and SaaS delivery without forcing every workload into the same architecture.

InspectableExpose state, validation, and system behavior instead of hiding it.

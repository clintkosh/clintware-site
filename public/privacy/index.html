PRIVACY

What this site collects and why.

Information you submit

Clintware collects information you choose to provide, such as details included in an email, contact form, product inquiry, support request, or blog subscription.

Contact form

The contact form requires a name, email address, and message. A phone number is optional. Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit for delivery to Clintware by email. FormSubmit states that submissions may be retained for up to 30 days. Do not submit sensitive information that is not necessary for the conversation.

Blog updates

If you subscribe, Clintware stores your email address and confirmation status to send the build-note messages you requested. The address is not added until it is confirmed. Every update includes an unsubscribe path, and unsubscribed addresses are not sent future updates.

Website data

Hosting, analytics, advertising, email-delivery, form-processing, and security providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser details, timestamps, page activity, delivery events, and request logs to deliver, measure, protect, or fund the site.

Clintware uses Google Analytics to measure page use, referring source, campaign parameters such as UTM tags, booking-funnel events, internal navigation, and outbound destination domains or paths. Custom analytics events are intentionally limited to navigation and product-use metadata and do not send contact-form text, booking names, email addresses, meeting topics, or URL query strings as event properties.

Google AdSense code and an authorized ads.txt declaration may be present on Clintware content pages. Ad serving remains subject to Google site approval, regional consent requirements, and Google publisher policies.

How contact information is used

Information submitted by email or through the contact form is used to respond to your request and manage the related conversation. Clintware does not sell your contact information to advertisers.

Product and linked-service boundaries

Some Clintware tools, alphas, or linked services may have different data paths or third-party dependencies. Product pages should state relevant current boundaries, and linked third-party services have their own privacy practices. Review those services before submitting sensitive information.

Google connected services

When you choose to connect a Google account, Clintware requests only the Google permissions needed for the feature you are using. Core Clintware connected-service functions may use your basic Google account identity (such as account identifier and verified email), Gmail permission to send messages you direct Clintware to send, and Google Calendar permissions to check free/busy availability and create, view, update, or cancel calendar events used by the scheduling feature.

The core delegated connection does not read or copy the contents of your Gmail mailbox. Free/busy results are used to determine scheduling availability and are not retained as a mailbox-style history. Calendar event information is used to provide the scheduling actions you request. Email content sent through Gmail is used only to deliver the message or notification requested by the relevant Clintware feature.

For delegated Google access, Clintware stores the minimum connection information needed to keep the authorized feature working, including the authorized Google account identifier/email and an encrypted Google refresh credential. Short-lived Google access tokens are obtained when needed and are not intentionally persisted as long-term application records. OAuth status diagnostics are retained only for limited operational troubleshooting. You can revoke Clintware access at any time through your Google Account, and you may request deletion of stored Clintware connection data by emailing clint@clintware.com.

Google user data is not sold, used for targeted advertising, provided to data brokers, or used to train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. Clintware uses Google user data only to provide or improve the user-facing connected feature you authorized. Data is disclosed only to Google and infrastructure/service providers as necessary to operate, secure, and deliver that feature, or when required by law.

Stored delegated credentials are protected using encrypted application storage and HTTPS transport, with provider secrets and refresh credentials kept behind Clintware's authorization boundary rather than exposed to client applications. Clintware's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

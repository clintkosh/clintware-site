Hyper Sprint

When the runway gets short, optimize for speed to signed offer.

Hyper Sprint is a candidate-controlled operating mode. It scores the current pipeline, detects when the search has become fragile, re-scans every active opportunity, creates a refill target, and ranks the next actions by expected speed-to-offer rather than title prestige.

Decision rules

GREEN

At least three healthy processes, at least one late-stage process, and recent movement. Keep compensation discipline and concentrate on converting what is already alive.

YELLOW / RED

The system increases refill pressure, flags stale processes, raises immediate-start and contract/CTH options, and pushes direct follow-up. RED is designed for fewer than seven days remaining without credible late-stage momentum.

Working alpha

Three useful surfaces already work.

Evidence Mapper

Paste your resume/accomplishments and target job. LandThePlane extracts role requirements and maps them to proof you can actually defend, keeping missing evidence visible rather than inventing it.

Brief Builder

Create browser-local intermediary job-search updates, interview progress briefs, offer announcements, onboarding updates, 30/60/90 progress briefs, and ongoing accomplishment summaries.

Interview Review

Record shared desktop/system audio plus a separate candidate microphone when available, or upload a recording. AI transcription and review combine answer content and role fit with observable delivery signals.

Interview Review

Score the interview from more than a transcript.

Capture
Record live desktop/system audio and keep the candidate microphone separate when browser permissions allow.A separate candidate track reduces the risk of treating interviewer speech as candidate delivery. The browser may require a shared visual surface, but LandThePlane records only audio tracks.
Upload
Use an existing candidate-only or full/mixed interview recording.Mixed-speaker recordings remain useful for content review but are explicitly marked as less reliable for candidate-specific delivery coaching.
Measure
Combine transcript evidence with observable communication characteristics.Current signals include speaking pace, approximate silence ratio, volume dynamics, sampled pitch variation, clipping, and recording scope. These are coaching signals, not emotion or personality detection.
Coach
Return content score, role-fit score, directional delivery score, strengths, missed opportunities, fit evidence, delivery observations, and what to practice next.The system is candidate-side coaching and does not claim to predict an employer's hiring decision.
Career lifecycle

LAND → RAMP → OPERATE → IMPROVE → PROVE → PIVOT

Land

Evidence map, answer compression, interview preparation, actual-interview review, opportunity tracking, and intermediary search briefs.

Ramp

After the offer, turn the job description into version 0 of a 30/60/90 success plan, then replace assumptions with actual work expectations and feedback.

Operate + Improve

Future opt-in email and meeting connections can turn assignments, commitments, stakeholders, decisions, and repeated processes into a personal work graph and workflow system.

Prove + Pivot

Accumulate verified impact for reviews and promotions, then use capability adjacency to surface credible next roles that title-based career searches may miss.

Career discovery

Find roles your job titles do not advertise.

Observe
Reason from demonstrated capability instead of title similarity.Use verified accomplishments, repeated workflows, learning velocity, feedback, preferences, and real work patterns as evidence.
Discover
Surface adjacent and non-obvious roles with an explainable fit.Every future pivot recommendation should show what evidence transfers and why a conventional title-based search might miss the role.
Measure
Separate proven strengths from skill and credibility gaps.Show what is proven, what is inferred, what must be learned, and what portfolio or domain proof would make the move believable.

Local-first where practical

The evidence mapper, profile setup, optional image use, Brief Builder rendering, and interview acoustic measurements can run in the browser. Saving remains explicit.

Explicit AI review

Interview audio is sent for transcription/review only after the user clicks Analyze interview. The current review Worker does not add persistent LandThePlane storage for audio, transcripts, or review output.

Connected accounts later

Authentication and data access remain separate permission decisions. Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, and meeting sources should be requested only for a feature the user explicitly turns on.

Current boundary: the evidence mapper, local Brief Builder, and dedicated interview-review pipeline are working alpha features. Persistent Career Graph accounts, post-hire connected work learning, automatic personal-workflow building, longitudinal interview history, and career-pivot recommendations remain in development.

Interview scores are directional coaching aids, not validated hiring or psychological assessments. Recording quality, conferencing software, multiple speakers, and incomplete context can affect results. Users are responsible for obtaining any required permission to record.

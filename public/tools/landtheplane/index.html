LandThePlane

And Hit the Ground Rolling.

LandThePlane is being built as a continuous career operating system. It starts by turning your verified experience and a target role into interview preparation. After you get hired, the same career graph becomes a ramp and workflow system that learns the real job from user-approved work context. Over time, it can also identify credible career pivots that ordinary title-based searching may miss, show why they fit, and build the shortest realistic ramp into them.

Career lifecycle

Land → Ramp → Operate → Improve → Prove → Pivot.

Land

Map a target role to evidence you can defend, then turn that proof into interview questions, concise answers, STAR retrieval, and a stage-specific prep plan.

Ramp

Once hired, turn the job description into version 0 of a 30/60/90-day success plan, then replace assumptions with what the job actually teaches you.

Operate + Improve

Learn assignments, stakeholders, decisions, recurring work, terminology, and feedback from approved sources such as email and meeting intelligence, then help convert repetition into personal workflows.

Prove + Pivot

Continuously capture evidence of impact for reviews and promotions, then use the same graph to reveal credible adjacent or unconventional roles and the gaps to close before moving.

Career-pivot engine

Find the roles your job titles do not advertise.

Observe
Reason from demonstrated capability rather than title similarity.Use verified accomplishments, repeated workflows, learning velocity, feedback, preferences, and real work patterns as evidence.
Discover
Surface adjacent and non-obvious roles with an explainable fit.Every recommendation should show the evidence that transfers and why the fit might be invisible in a normal keyword search.
Measure
Separate proven strengths from credibility gaps.Show skill distance, missing tools or domain knowledge, portfolio gaps, and a confidence level rather than pretending every suggestion is equally realistic.
Ramp
Build the shortest credible path into the new role.Create a learning plan, proof-building projects, bridge language, and role-specific interview preparation using the same ramp engine used after a new hire.
Work graph

The product gets more useful after the offer.

Email as the primary growth source

With explicit permission, extract assignments, commitments, deadlines, stakeholders, recurring requests, decisions, terminology, feedback, and success signals. The durable database is the structured work graph, not a permanent raw-mailbox copy.

Meetings as operating context

Meeting sources such as Read AI can contribute action items, topics, questions, participants, decisions, and feedback. Summaries and action items should be preferred before full transcript ingestion.

Your corrections train your system

Accepted and rejected suggestions, edits, explicit feedback, and observed corrections teach LandThePlane how you work. Personal preferences remain distinguishable from employer policy.

Evidence system

One graph connects interviews, performance, and future moves.

Prepare
Requirement/evidence map, likely questions, answer cards, STAR bank, gaps, and questions to ask.Generated factual claims remain traceable to evidence the user supplied or confirmed.
Perform
Stakeholder map, commitments, recurring workflows, manager 1:1 briefs, meeting-to-action conversion, and accomplishment capture.Work performed after hire becomes structured evidence rather than disappearing into inboxes and calendars.
Advance
Performance-review packs, promotion evidence, resume updates, internal mobility, and future interview preparation.The system can reuse verified impact instead of forcing the user to reconstruct a career narrative from memory every few years.

Local / standalone path

The current alpha performs evidence mapping in the browser. Future standalone storage is intended to preserve the same candidate-owned career/work graph on-device.

Optional SaaS path

Cloud sync can add cross-device history, connected sources, model-assisted preparation, ramp plans, workflow learning, and career discovery while retaining export, deletion, and source-level controls.

Connected-account boundary

Authentication and data access are separate decisions. Email and meeting connections should be opt-in, scoped, revocable, and designed to extract the minimum durable work context needed.

Current boundary: the public alpha is a working local interview evidence mapper. Post-hire ramping, connected work learning, workflow building, and career-pivot recommendations are product direction, not currently active alpha features.

Career suggestions must be evidence-backed and explainable. The system should expose what it inferred, what is actually proven, what remains a gap, and why it believes a non-obvious role is worth considering.

Turn verified career evidence into role-specific interview preparation.

LandThePlane starts with a resume or accomplishment source plus the role you are targeting. It maps requirements to evidence you can actually defend, then turns that evidence into concise answer formats, STAR retrieval, and stage-specific preparation. The longer-term product learns from your edits, interview rounds, and outcomes so the coaching gets more useful over time.

Product wedge

Evidence first. Generation second.

Map the role

Extract the requirements that matter from the actual job description and interview stage rather than starting from a generic question bank.

Map your proof

Connect those requirements to verified accomplishment evidence, ownership, metrics, and confidence. Missing proof remains visible instead of being invented.

Compress for retrieval

Reuse the same evidence as a one-sentence proof point, 30-second opening, 45–75 second answer, STAR story, panel talking point, or closing bridge.

Preparation system

One evidence graph, many interview surfaces.

Intake
Resume + target role + interview stage.Text works in the alpha. PDF/DOCX and job-link intake are planned for the SaaS build.
Prepare
Role map, likely questions, answer cards, STAR bank, gaps, and questions to ask.Full generated preparation will stay traceable to the candidate evidence underneath it.
Practice
Move from complete stories to compact answers without losing the facts.Preferred compression: POINT → PROOF → RESULT → ROLE LINK → STOP.
Learn
Use later rounds to improve the next round.Planned longitudinal coaching tracks what was asked, which evidence was used, what changed, and recurring weaknesses across interviews.

Local / standalone path

The current alpha performs its evidence mapping in the browser. Raw resume and job text are not uploaded to LandThePlane. Future standalone storage is intended to preserve the same canonical evidence/history model on-device.

Optional SaaS path

Cloud sync is planned for candidates who want history across devices, multiple target roles, exports, model-assisted prep, and longitudinal coaching. Cloud storage should be explicit rather than mandatory.

Google authentication

Google OAuth is not required for the MVP. If added, it should be optional sign-in only. Gmail, Drive, Calendar, and Contacts access are not part of the interview-prep wedge.

Current boundary: the public alpha is a useful local evidence mapper, not the finished AI coaching system. It does not promise interview outcomes and is not designed as a concealed live-answer feed. Public demos should use synthetic or explicitly approved material.

Name status: LandThePlane is being used as a working product name. Standalone trademark/name clearance remains a pre-paid-launch step; no trademark claim is being made here.

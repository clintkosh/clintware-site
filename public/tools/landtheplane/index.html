Prepare for the interview. Track the search. Land the role. Keep the career evidence growing.

LandThePlane starts with a resume or accomplishment source plus the role you are targeting. It maps requirements to evidence you can defend and compresses that proof into useful interview answers. The same product now includes a browser-local Astro-style Brief Builder for polished intermediary job-search updates, offer landing announcements, onboarding/ramp briefs, and ongoing accomplishment summaries.

Product wedge

Evidence first. Generation second.

Map the role

Extract the requirements that matter from the actual job description and interview stage rather than starting from a generic question bank.

Map your proof

Connect those requirements to verified accomplishment evidence, ownership, metrics, and confidence. Missing proof remains visible instead of being invented.

Compress for retrieval

Reuse the same evidence as a one-sentence proof point, 30-second opening, 45–75 second answer, STAR story, panel talking point, or closing bridge.

Brief Builder

Astro-style status updates across the entire career lifecycle.

Search

Generate intermediary job-search updates with issue numbers, top changes, full opportunity tables, probabilities, compensation notes, and next actions.

Land

Convert the final search state into an offer/landing announcement with a clean recap, transition tasks, and a coherent next-chapter message.

Ramp

Create first-week and 30/60/90-day briefs that track wins, stakeholders, commitments, learning, projects, and measurable progress.

Prove

Turn weekly or monthly work into career evidence that can later support 1:1s, reviews, promotions, resume updates, and future interview prep.

Personalized setup: users can add display details, a role/goal line, compensation context, a visual theme, and an optional profile image. In the alpha, those inputs remain browser-local unless the user explicitly saves them in local browser storage.

Local / standalone path

The current alpha performs evidence mapping, profile setup, optional image use, and Brief Builder rendering in the browser. Raw resume and job text are not uploaded to LandThePlane.

Optional SaaS path

Cloud sync is planned for candidates who want history across devices, connected sources, scheduled updates, model-assisted prep, and longitudinal coaching. Cloud storage should be explicit rather than mandatory.

Connected accounts

Authentication and data access remain separate permission decisions. Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, and meeting sources should be requested only for a feature the user explicitly turns on.

Current boundary: the public alpha is a local evidence mapper and local HTML Brief Builder, not the finished AI coaching and connected-data system. It does not promise employment outcomes and is not designed as a concealed live-answer feed. Public demos should use synthetic or explicitly approved material.

Name status: LandThePlane is being used as a working product name. Standalone trademark/name clearance remains a pre-paid-launch step; no trademark claim is being made here.

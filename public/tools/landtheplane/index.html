LandThePlane

And Hit the Ground Rolling.

A career operating system that keeps verified evidence useful from one interview through the next job.

Most job-search tools reset every time the user opens a new application. The resume is pasted again, the job description is pasted again, generic answers are generated again, and useful evidence from earlier interviews disappears. LandThePlane is being built around the opposite idea: the durable asset is the candidate's verified evidence. Roles, interviews, feedback, wins, gaps, and later work history should accumulate around that evidence instead of replacing it.

Status: working alpha. LandThePlane is being prepared as a Fall 2026 Y Combinator application candidate alongside Quillgeist. It is not presented as accepted by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Y Combinator.

Overview

The product is not “AI interview questions.”

The core is a candidate-owned evidence system that can be reused across a career.

Problem
Career evidence is fragmented and most AI career tools treat every application as a fresh chat.Users repeatedly rebuild context from resumes, notes, old interviews, job descriptions, performance history, and memory. That wastes time and makes it easy for generated answers to drift beyond what the candidate can actually defend.
Product
Build a persistent evidence layer first, then use it for preparation and decisions.Requirements from a target role are mapped to verified accomplishments. Missing proof stays visible. Interview review adds actual performance data instead of pretending preparation ended when the call started.
Compounding value
The same evidence should become more useful after every interview and after the job is won.Interview history, feedback, accomplishments, 30/60/90 progress, and later work outcomes can strengthen the same career record instead of living in separate documents.
Boundary
Evidence and coaching, not fake certainty.LandThePlane should show what is proven, inferred, or missing. Interview scores are directional coaching aids, not hiring predictions or psychological assessments.
What exists now

Three working surfaces.

These are current alpha capabilities. The larger Career Graph vision remains under development.

EVIDENCE MAPPERMap resume/accomplishment evidence against the requirements of a target role without inventing missing proof.
BRIEF BUILDERCreate structured search, interview, offer, onboarding, and accomplishment briefs in the browser.
INTERVIEW REVIEWRecord or upload interview audio, transcribe it, review answer content and role fit, and add observable delivery signals.
Interview capture
Live desktop/system audio plus a separate candidate microphone when browser permissions allow.Keeping the candidate track separate improves delivery coaching and avoids treating interviewer speech as candidate behavior. Uploaded candidate-only or mixed recordings are also supported.
Delivery signals
Observable recording characteristics supplement the transcript.Current signals include pace, approximate silence ratio, volume dynamics, sampled pitch variation, clipping, and recording scope. They are communication-coaching inputs, not emotion or personality detection.
Privacy direction
Keep local work local where practical and make cloud analysis explicit.Evidence mapping, Brief Builder rendering, and acoustic measurements can run in the browser. Interview audio is sent for transcription/review only after the user chooses to analyze it.
Wedge

Start with the moment where the user has urgency and measurable feedback: the interview.

The larger career operating system is only credible if the first use case creates repeat behavior.

Before
Prepare from evidence the candidate can defend.Map the role, compress useful stories, identify gaps, and keep the candidate from memorizing generic AI prose.
During / after
Review the interview that actually happened.Use transcript evidence, target-role requirements, and observable delivery characteristics to identify what landed, what was missed, and what to practice before the next round.
Across rounds
Do not restart from zero.Carry verified evidence, questions, feedback, and corrections forward so the next round is informed by the previous one.
After hire
Preserve the evidence system instead of throwing it away.The roadmap extends the same record into onboarding, work commitments, accomplishments, performance evidence, promotion preparation, and future career moves.
What is not finished

The broader Career Graph is the direction, not a shipped claim.

Persistent accounts
Longitudinal career history across devices and sessions.Current local-first surfaces work without pretending the full persistent account model is complete.
Connected work
Opt-in email, calendar, meeting, and work-history connections after hire.Those permissions should be requested feature by feature rather than treating access to a user's working life as a default.
Career discovery
Surface credible adjacent roles from demonstrated capability, not title similarity alone.Future recommendations should explain what evidence transfers, what is inferred, and what proof or learning is still needed.
Longitudinal coaching
Measure whether answers and delivery improve across rounds.This becomes valuable only when enough real interview history exists to compare performance without fabricating confidence.
YC case

LandThePlane has a clearer wedge than many career tools, but it is entering a crowded market.

The Fall 2026 application case depends on evidence that the saved career record creates repeat use that generic resume + job-description tools do not.

Repeat use
Candidates return across interview rounds and across multiple roles.One-time resume analysis is not enough. The evidence layer should become more valuable because the user has already invested in it.
Evidence reuse
A meaningful portion of preparation comes from previously verified evidence rather than rebuilding the candidate profile.This is the compounding behavior the product thesis depends on.
Outcome signal
Users report clearer preparation, faster iteration, and better ability to explain their actual experience.The product should improve the candidate's process without claiming control over the employer's hiring decision.
Commercial pull
Users are willing to pay, refer another candidate, or keep using the system after the immediate interview.That is more meaningful than traffic generated by a free interview-prep page.
Current product rule: LandThePlane should not be promoted above Quillgeist simply because the career wedge is easier to explain. External repeat use, evidence reuse across rounds, paid pull, referrals, and post-interview retention should decide whether it becomes the stronger Fall 2026 YC case.

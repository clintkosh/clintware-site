Different by design

Not an AI cofounder persona.

The first proof focuses on the part most founder copilots skip: who is allowed to do what, where work should execute, what proof closes a task, and what remains under founder control.

Authority before autonomy

Every generated action is assigned an authority ceiling. The MVP only executes reversible local simulation; external actions remain review-gated.

Local-first routing

Assemblerer decides whether a task belongs on-device or in a hosted model based on sensitivity, complexity, and the founder's routing preference.

Evidence closes work

Tasks are defined with expected proof, not vague completion. That creates an audit-friendly operating trail that can later connect to AgentBridge, MCP, SaaS, or local tools.

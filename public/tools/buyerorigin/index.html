Buyer identity and promotion eligibility for commerce.

BuyerOrigin explores buyer identity and promotion-eligibility infrastructure for merchants, using repeated “new customer” discount abuse as the initial wedge. The goal is to help merchants recognize eligibility patterns even when superficial account identifiers change.

This remains an exploratory product track. Quillgeist is Clintware™’s flagship startup product.

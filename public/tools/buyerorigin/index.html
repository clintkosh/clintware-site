BuyerOrigin

Protect the offer, not the checkout. BuyerOrigin helps merchants decide whether the underlying buyer is actually eligible for an acquisition offer, even when accounts or superficial identifiers change.

Working: local CSV audit, merchant policy controls, identity linkage, explainable eligibility, overrides, leakage estimate, exportNot shipped: Shopify install, billing, live offer enforcement

Current W27 decision: BuyerOrigin is the primary YC candidate because it has the shortest route to hard economic evidence. Quillgeist remains the close technical alternate.
Merchant policy
The merchant defines what counts as eligibility.The working engine supports a 2-of-3 or 3-of-3 identity threshold, configurable lookback, same-code or acquisition-wide scope, and an allowed-prior-redemption count.
Identity evidence
Link the buyer without pretending one identifier is identity.BuyerOrigin normalizes email aliases, phone numbers, and shipping addresses, names the matching signals, and points to the prior qualifying order.
Decision boundary
Eligibility is separate from payment authorization.Monitor mode produces a review queue. Enforcement simulation can deny the promotional entitlement only. Checkout is never denied by this MVP.
Agentic commerce
Preserve the channel without declaring the channel fraudulent.Optional actor and shopping-agent identifiers are retained in the decision record. Automation alone does not make an order ineligible.
Merchant override
Human judgment remains visible.A merchant override can allow an offer while keeping the identity evidence and policy result in the record for later accuracy analysis.

What must be proven next

Demand
Complete real merchant audits.Target at least three real-data audits, with merchant-confirmed questionable redemptions and a quantified dollar impact.
Accuracy
Measure confirmed eligibility errors, overrides, false positives, review time, and missed cases.No automated production claim is justified until merchants review the decisions.
Commercial
Get an explicit paid commitment.The W27 proof is not the number of flags. It is merchant-confirmed acquisition leakage and willingness to pay to stop it.
Platform path
Keep Shopify as the wedge, not the ceiling.The broader thesis is merchant-controlled offer eligibility for human and agentic commerce. Live Shopify enforcement still requires the appropriate privacy, API, Function-state, and protected-data work.

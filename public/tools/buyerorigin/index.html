BuyerOrigin

A promotion-eligibility system for merchants, starting with repeated new-customer offer use. The working MVP audits Shopify-style order exports locally and flags only records that match an earlier redemption on two or more buyer signals.

Working: CSV audit, reason codes, allowlist, leakage estimate, exportNot shipped: Shopify install, billing, live discount enforcement

Portfolio decision: BuyerOrigin is number two overall and number one for immediate commercial validation. Quillgeist remains the primary Winter 2027 YC pitch until BuyerOrigin produces stronger external evidence from real merchants.
Merchant problem
The same buyer can repeatedly consume an acquisition offer after superficial identifiers change.The merchant pays to acquire a customer it may already have, but a careless fraud rule can also punish legitimate households.
Current product
A local, explainable historical audit.BuyerOrigin normalizes email aliases, phone numbers, and shipping addresses, applies a two-signal rule, identifies the prior matched order, and exports a review queue. Order data stays in browser memory.
Decision boundary
Protect the offer, not the checkout.Monitor mode recommends review. Enforcement simulation recommends denying only the discount. A merchant can allowlist a legitimate record without deleting the evidence.

What must be proven next

Demand
Three merchants must complete real-data audits.The validation target is at least 10 suspicious redemptions per month or plausible monthly leakage of $250 or more, plus willingness to consider $39 to $99 per month.
Accuracy
Every flag needs merchant review before automation.Track confirmed abuse, false positives, override rate, review time, and missed cases. No production claim is justified until those results exist.
Shopify path
Use a hosted control plane plus compact precomputed Function state.Ordinary Function execution should not assume live third-party network access. Protected customer-data approval, privacy webhooks, scope minimization, and current API-limit tests are preconditions.
YC trigger
BuyerOrigin becomes pitch number one only if merchant evidence overtakes Quillgeist by October 19.The trigger is three completed audits, two merchants confirming material value, at least one paid commitment, and acceptable error and override rates.

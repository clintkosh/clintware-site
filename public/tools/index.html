PRODUCT REGISTER · CLINTWARE™

What is being built, what works now, and what is still only a thesis.

Clintware is not presenting every experiment as a startup. This page is the current product record. Quillgeist is the flagship. LandThePlane is the fast-track challenger. The remaining products are working utilities, supporting experiments, or product tracks that stay secondary until user behavior says otherwise.

Fall 2026 YC plan: Quillgeist and LandThePlane are application candidates. Neither is presented as accepted, endorsed, or affiliated with Y Combinator.

Startup priority

These are the two products being actively evaluated for a Fall 2026 Y Combinator application.

Quillgeist
Flagship · Public alpha
Adaptive intent compiler and execution layer for AI.Working proof: paired local nodes, controlled local execution, cloud dispatch, tests, Git/process/file operations, snapshots, rollback, and inspectable result evidence. Larger thesis: understand normal user intent, improve it for the target model/tool, execute eligible work locally, and learn from corrections over time. Adaptive learning remains in development.

LandThePlane
YC challenger · Working alpha
Candidate-owned career evidence system with interview prep as the initial wedge.Working proof: evidence mapping, Brief Builder, and live/uploaded interview review. The company thesis is not generic AI interview questions; it is evidence that compounds across rounds, roles, onboarding, accomplishments, and future career moves.

Product track · Early build

Pre-exposure email security that is not tied to one provider.

ShoulderSoldier is a provider-independent security layer intended to inspect and protect sensitive email content before exposure to the downstream email or AI provider.

Product pageContact Clintware
Initial thesis

Move useful security decisions earlier.

Design around the user and policy boundary rather than depending on a single mail platform. Use local inspection and explicit policy where appropriate so sensitive content does not have to reach a provider before it can be protected.

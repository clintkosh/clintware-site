Promotion path

PROBLEM → EXPERIMENT → TOOL → PRODUCT

I give an experiment stronger status when the problem repeats, the solution remains useful outside its original context, and outside evidence supports continued investment. Newness alone is not evidence.

Why I keep the separation.

I can build quickly without presenting every prototype as a new SaaS company. Products remain credible, experiments remain honest, and useful one-off tools can stay useful without being forced into a venture narrative.

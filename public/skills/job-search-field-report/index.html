Job Search Field Report ASTRO

A reusable operating method for recurring job-search reports. It separates lifetime funnel totals from what actually changed since the last report was sent, reconciles active and closed hiring processes, and treats sending as a separate authorized action.

The rule that prevents report drift

difference_window = (latest_sent_report_at, current_at]

A report draft does not advance the comparison clock. The next report begins when the previous report was actually sent, whether that interval is six hours, nine days, or three weeks. This prevents arbitrary weekly or two-week summaries from quietly mixing old and new activity.

What it tracks

  1. Cumulative application and interview funnel totals.
  2. Exact since-last-sent advances, closures, applications, interviews, referrals, and follow-ups.
  3. An evidence-backed active pipeline rather than every open application.
  4. Closed-process history so rejected or filled roles do not remain visually active.
  5. Verified vs estimated vs unknown compensation.
  6. Relevant certifications, portfolio work, and proof of skill development.

What it refuses to do

It does not guess the comparison period from “two weeks ago,” treat a saved draft as a sent update, inflate interview counts from duplicate messages, leave closed roles in the active pipeline, or turn estimated compensation into employer-confirmed compensation.

Standalone by design

The workflow is not tied to Clintware, Gmail, Google Calendar, a specific ATS, or a specific tracker. Those can be adapters when available. The core skill works from supplied evidence, and the sample report is plain static HTML with no framework, database, account, or application dependency.

Public version intentionally removes private names, email addresses, real hiring companies, personal job-search metrics, and private project details. The presentation is deliberately neutral and startup-oriented; it does not use or imply Y Combinator branding or endorsement.

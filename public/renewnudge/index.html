WHY RENEWNUDGE

Risk pipeline, not a feeling.

Explain the health score

Use adoption, executive engagement, ticket pressure, escalations, onboarding, touch recency, and renewal proximity as visible weighted inputs instead of hiding risk behind a black box.

Prioritize attention

Move accounts into operational lanes such as Monitor, Needs Outreach, Escalated, Renewal Save, and Stable with severity, owner, and next action attached.

Turn signals into motion

Apply repeatable playbooks for onboarding, renewal saves, EBRs, escalations, expansion, and other post-sale moments rather than rebuilding the response every time.

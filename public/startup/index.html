Why Mind to Form moved to #1.

Its wedge is not “text to CAD.” The product begins where generative systems often fail: establishing what the human actually means by finished. The approved Definition of Done becomes the contract controlling design, revision, manufacturability, sourcing, and eventually ordering. The immediate test is compact and falsifiable: can outside users describe an object nontechnically, approve the inferred definition, receive a useful first draft, and take that package to a real prototype?

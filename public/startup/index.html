Y COMBINATOR W27 · WINTER 2027 · VALIDATION RACE

BuyerOrigin is the current W27 primary. Quillgeist is close enough to take it back with stronger usage evidence.

Clintware is not submitting multiple YC applications. The portfolio is being used to force one evidence-based choice: BuyerOrigin wins by proving merchant economics; Quillgeist wins by proving portable user behavior; RenewNudge and ShoulderSoldier move up only if their external evidence overtakes both.

#1 · YC primary · Working MVP

BuyerOrigin

Merchant-controlled offer eligibility. The product links repeat buyer identity signals and decides whether an acquisition offer is still available under the merchant's own policy, without treating payment authorization as the same problem.

Working: local CSV audit; configurable identity threshold; lookback window; acquisition-wide or same-code scope; allowed prior uses; explainable eligibility; merchant overrides; agent/channel context; leakage estimate; export.Must prove: real merchant data, merchant-confirmed leakage, acceptable override/false-positive rates, and at least one paid commitment.
BuyerOrigin →
#2 · Technical alternate · Core v0.4

Quillgeist

User-owned operating context and verified local execution across AI systems. The product keeps operating rules outside one provider, compiles only the relevant subset, executes eligible deterministic work locally, verifies results, and proposes rather than silently learns persistent rules.

Working: global/project/task rules; keyword gating; selective compilation; context-avoidance telemetry; correction proposals; local execution; allow/ask/never; verification; rollback; Contextor; Assemblerer manifest compilation.Must prove: outside multi-model users, repeated-instruction reduction, external-context reduction, verified completion, and meaningful provider portability.
Quillgeist →
#3 · Technical Customer Success · Live product

RenewNudge

Technical SaaS renewal intelligence. Instead of one opaque account score, the current model separates product adoption, support pressure, stakeholder engagement, and technical readiness, then uses the weakest dimension to drive an explainable next action.

Working: existing account/renewal workflow plus explainable four-dimension readiness; direct technical/stakeholder custom fields when supplied; explicit derived fallback when they are absent; risk-stage and next-action prioritization.Must prove: design-partner usage, risk visibility that changes CSM behavior, renewal outcomes or earlier intervention, and willingness to pay in technical/cybersecurity SaaS.
RenewNudge →
#4 · Security watchlist · Working heuristic MVP

ShoulderSoldier

User-interaction firewall for social engineering across email, browser, messaging, and AI-agent requested actions. The current evaluator produces explainable risk signals and proportionate friction without claiming a production ML detector.

Working: four interaction channels; deterministic risk signals; URL/attachment checks; credential/payment/execution/exfiltration/authority signals; agent external-action friction; risk tiers and recommendations.Must prove: value on labeled real interactions, false-positive behavior, integration at the point of action, and user tolerance for interventions.
ShoulderSoldier →
Supporting product proof

LandThePlane

Candidate-owned career evidence system with working evidence mapping, Brief Builder, and interview review. It remains an active product, but the current recruiting/career-agent market makes it a weaker W27 application than the four tracks above.

Must prove: repeat use across stages and durable value from the evidence graph.
LandThePlane →
Current decision rule: BuyerOrigin stays #1 if merchant audits produce confirmed economic value and payment intent. Quillgeist becomes #1 if outside users demonstrate portable operating rules, materially less repeated instruction/context, and high verified completion across multiple AI providers. Rankings change on evidence, not because a product is newer or more fashionable.

WINTER 2027 · DECISION LOCK OCTOBER 19

Quillgeist is the primary YC pitch. BuyerOrigin is the number two challenger.

This order follows current product proof. It can change once, on October 19, if verified merchant evidence for BuyerOrigin overtakes Quillgeist's repeat-user evidence. It will not change during the final application polish.

#1 · YC primary · Public alpha

Quillgeist

User-owned runtime and memory for AI agents. It applies local permissions and explicit saved preferences, executes eligible work on the user's machine, verifies the result, and returns compact evidence to the model.

Working: packaged Windows, macOS, and Linux nodes; local execution; cloud pairing; policies; tests; snapshots; rollback; result evidence; explicit persistent preferences.Must prove: repeated external use and measurable improvement across providers.
Quillgeist →
#2 · Commercial challenger · MVP

BuyerOrigin

Promotion eligibility for Shopify merchants. The working local audit flags repeated limited-offer use only when two or more buyer signals match, explains the reasons, supports merchant overrides, and estimates leakage.

Working: browser-local audit, sample data, CSV upload/paste, reason codes, monitor mode, enforcement simulation, allowlist, and export.Must prove: three merchant audits, material confirmed leakage, acceptable false positives, and at least one paid commitment.
BuyerOrigin →
#3 · Active career track

LandThePlane

Candidate-owned career evidence system. Interview preparation is the initial wedge; the durable value is reusable evidence across applications, interviews, hiring, and later work.

Working: evidence mapping, Brief Builder, and interview review.Must prove: repeat use across stages and continued value from the evidence graph.
LandThePlane →
Decision rule: BuyerOrigin becomes number one only if three merchants complete audits, two confirm material value, at least one commits to pay, and the observed error and override rates are acceptable. Otherwise Quillgeist remains the single Winter 2027 application story.

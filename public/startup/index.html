FALL 2026 STARTUP MEMO

Two YC application candidates. One has to earn priority through evidence.

Quillgeist is the current flagship. LandThePlane is the fast-track challenger. Both are being prepared as possible Fall 2026 Y Combinator application candidates. This page records the actual thesis, current proof, and what still has to be learned before either should be presented as a company with conviction.

Status language is deliberate: candidate / preparing to apply. Clintware does not claim YC acceptance, affiliation, endorsement, interview selection, or funding.
01 · Current flagship

Quillgeist

Read product brief →

Quillgeist starts from a repeated problem in AI work: people have to restate intent, constraints, tools, preferences, and definitions of done, while models are often asked to supervise deterministic work a local computer could perform directly. The product thesis is an adaptive intent compiler that understands what the user means, improves the instruction for the target model/tool, executes eligible work locally, verifies the result, and eventually learns from corrections.

Proof now
Working local execution and cloud control.Windows, macOS, and Linux node builds; paired machines; file/process execution; Python, PowerShell, Node.js and Git; tests; schedules; snapshots; rollback; local capability policy; result evidence.
Unproven core
Adaptive learning has to reduce repeated instruction in real use.The larger company thesis is not proven by a local executor alone. Accepted edits, rejected edits, preferences, corrections, and task outcomes need to measurably improve future execution.
YC evidence
Repeated meaningful delegation, local leverage, and trust.Users should repeatedly give Quillgeist real work, allow controlled execution because the permission model is understandable, and experience lower friction or cloud overhead without worse outcomes.
02 · Fast-track challenger

LandThePlane

Read product brief →

LandThePlane begins with interview preparation because the user has urgency and feedback, but the product is not another AI question generator. The durable asset is a candidate-owned evidence system: verified accomplishments, role requirements, interview history, feedback, later work evidence, and credible career pivots that accumulate instead of resetting for every application.

Proof now
Evidence Mapper, Brief Builder, and interview review.Users can map defendable evidence against a role, create structured career/search briefs, and review live or uploaded interview audio using transcript evidence plus observable delivery signals.
Market risk
Interview prep is crowded and easy to copy superficially.The product only becomes defensible if saved evidence compounds across rounds and roles, users return instead of restarting, and the post-interview record remains valuable after the immediate job search.
YC evidence
Repeat use, evidence reuse, referrals, payment, and post-interview retention.A good demo or useful one-time answer is not enough. The evidence graph has to become something users do not want to rebuild elsewhere.

How priority changes

No product becomes flagship because its page sounds better or because a new idea feels easier to explain.

Usage
Repeated behavior beats signup volume.Measure tasks delegated, rounds reused, active users returning, and whether the product becomes part of an actual workflow.
Pull
Payment, referrals, design-partner requests, and unsolicited return use matter.These are stronger signals than compliments or one-time curiosity.
Wedge
The first use case must lead naturally into the larger thesis.Quillgeist must prove that local execution and intent learning belong together. LandThePlane must prove that interview preparation creates a durable evidence graph rather than a disposable prep session.
Kill signal
Be willing to demote a product when behavior does not support the thesis.If users do not return, do not reuse saved state, or do not trust the product with meaningful work, the product should not be dressed up as a YC company.

Secondary product tracks

ShoulderSoldier, BuyerOrigin, RenewNudge, Assemblerer, QuotaWatch, and the smaller Clintware utilities remain visible as proof of range, but they do not compete for attention with the two Fall 2026 candidates unless outside evidence changes that.

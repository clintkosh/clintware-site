What is easy to miss

Some of the most valuable work is the problem that never becomes visible.

A quiet account does not always mean nothing is happening. It can mean risk was caught early, a technical issue was contained before it became an escalation, a stakeholder concern was resolved before it became an executive complaint, or Support, Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success were aligned before the customer ever felt the friction.

Preventive work

The best escalation can be the one that never has to happen.

I watch for changes in usage, support patterns, stakeholder behavior, commitments, communication cadence, technical friction, and business context. The point is not to create activity. It is to intervene early enough that the customer experiences continuity instead of a fire.

When a fire already exists

Contain it, create one narrative, and close the loop.

I establish impact, bring in the right owners, keep communication clear, separate signal from noise, drive the next decision, and then use what happened to improve the process so the same failure mode is less likely to repeat.

The work customers often never see

A quiet account is not always an inactive account. It can be the result of risk caught early, a technical issue routed before escalation, a stakeholder concern handled before executive impact, or a recurring failure converted into a playbook before the next customer encounters it.

Weak signals
Support demand, usage movement, stakeholder participation, response cadence, missed commitments, recurring friction.Those changes can surface risk earlier than a renewal date or a static health score.
Escalations
One customer narrative, clear internal owners, controlled communication.Separate immediate recovery from root-cause prevention, keep specialists focused on the fix, and keep the customer informed without conflicting messages.

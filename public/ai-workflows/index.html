AI WORKFLOWS · PRACTICAL EXECUTION

Automate repetition. Keep judgment visible.

I use AI and automation to reduce coordination work, structure messy inputs, and speed up repeatable tasks. The useful question is not whether AI can touch a workflow. It is where automation improves the outcome without hiding evidence or accountability.

Operating rules

Four constraints I use before automating a workflow.

01 · Definition

Know what “done” means.

Automation is easier to trust when success, failure, and required evidence are explicit.

02 · Boundary

Separate deterministic work from judgment.

Formatting, routing, retrieval, checks, and repetitive execution can often be automated before decision-making is.

03 · Evidence

Return what changed.

A useful workflow should make inputs, actions, outputs, and exceptions inspectable.

04 · Review

Keep a human in the consequential loop.

Customer commitments, sensitive communications, and material business decisions should not disappear behind an opaque suggestion.

AI WORKFLOW OPERATING RECORD

Automate the repeated work. Keep consequential judgment inspectable.

I use AI when it removes coordination, retrieval, formatting, comparison, or execution work without hiding who owns the decision. The point is not to put AI into every step. The point is to make the operating system faster while preserving evidence, privacy boundaries, and accountability.

The decision test before I automate something

Definition of done
Can success and failure be stated clearly enough to check?If nobody can say what a correct result looks like, automation only makes ambiguity faster.
Deterministic vs judgment
Which parts are repeatable execution and which parts require context or responsibility?Retrieval, formatting, routing, comparison, tests, and repetitive local actions are often candidates for automation. Customer commitments, material business decisions, and sensitive communication usually require explicit review.
Evidence
What should be returned so another person can verify what happened?Changed files, source records, calculations, exceptions, tests, timestamps, ownership, and next actions are more useful than an opaque “completed” response.
Data boundary
Does this work need to leave the local or controlled system at all?Keep routine/local work local where practical. Send only the context a model actually needs, especially when customer, company, or personal data is involved.

What I do not treat as progress

AI theater
Adding a model to a deterministic workflow simply so the product can say “AI.”If normal code is safer, cheaper, easier to inspect, and equally effective, use normal code.
Invisible recommendation
A score or suggestion with no source trail, owner, or reason to trust it.AI-assisted systems should make it easier to inspect why an action is being considered, not harder.
Permission creep
Connecting email, calendar, files, or customer data before a feature actually needs them.Request access for the specific workflow the user enables. Do not make broad access the price of entering the product.
Roadmap-as-demo
Describing future learning, autonomy, prediction, or integrations as if they already work.Current capability and future direction stay separate on Clintware pages and in product discussions.

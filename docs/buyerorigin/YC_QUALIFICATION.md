# BuyerOrigin YC qualification

Status: current number-one Winter 2027 YC validation track as of September 7, 2026.  Quillgeist remains the close technical alternate.

## Current recommendation

BuyerOrigin holds the primary position because the product is working, the economic question is concrete, and the shortest path to stronger YC evidence is now external merchant validation rather than additional feature breadth.  Quillgeist has deeper technical breadth and can retake the primary position if it produces stronger repeated outside-user evidence first.

The ranking is evidence-based and can change before the internal application lock.

## Keep-primary trigger

BuyerOrigin should remain the primary YC pitch if, before the final application lock:

- at least three merchants complete real-data audits;
- at least two confirm material leakage;
- at least one becomes paid or signs a clear paid pilot commitment;
- the false-positive and override rates are acceptable to those merchants; and
- the Shopify integration plan survives a current API and privacy review.

If those signals do not appear while Quillgeist demonstrates repeated multi-provider use and measurable reduction in repeated instruction/context, revisit the order rather than defending BuyerOrigin by inertia.

## Matter-of-fact YC description

BuyerOrigin helps Shopify merchants stop the same buyer from repeatedly using new-customer promotions.  It compares permitted buyer signals, requires multiple matches, explains every decision, and denies the discount rather than the checkout.

## Current evidence

- Working browser-local CSV audit.
- Tested two-signal rule, reason codes, allowlist, and export.
- Configurable identity threshold, lookback, acquisition-vs-code scope, and prior-use policy.
- Merchant overrides, agent/channel context, and leakage estimates.
- Hosted BuyerOrigin product surface and custom-domain deployment path.
- Architecture, pilot, privacy, and security boundaries documented.

## Missing evidence

- External merchant audits.
- Paid usage or a clear paid pilot commitment.
- Measured false-positive and override rates on real merchant data.
- Live Shopify enforcement or connected monitor.
- Demonstrated retention or repeat workflow use.

## Immediate work

Do not use the primary validation window to add speculative features.  Recruit qualified Shopify merchants, run date-bounded CSV reviews locally, record confirmed leakage and false positives, present the merchant readout, and ask for paid continuation.  Build connected Shopify enforcement only when the audit evidence justifies it.

The existing pilot playbook targets three merchants, at least two with meaningful recoverable value, and at least one willing to pay.  Those are the next gates that matter.

# BuyerOrigin YC qualification

Status: portfolio challenger, not the primary Winter 2027 YC application as of September 6, 2026.

## Recommendation

BuyerOrigin should be number two overall and number one in the merchant commercial-validation lane.  Quillgeist remains number one for the Winter 2027 YC application because it has the deeper working product, packaged releases, a public alpha, and a coherent founder-problem connection.  BuyerOrigin can overtake it only through stronger external evidence.

## Promotion trigger

Move BuyerOrigin to the primary YC pitch if, before the final application lock:

- at least three merchants complete real-data audits;
- at least two confirm material leakage;
- at least one becomes paid or signs a clear paid pilot commitment;
- the false-positive and override rates are acceptable to those merchants; and
- the Shopify integration plan survives a current API and privacy review.

## Matter-of-fact YC description

BuyerOrigin helps Shopify merchants stop the same buyer from repeatedly using new-customer promotions.  It compares permitted buyer signals, requires multiple matches, explains every decision, and denies the discount rather than the checkout.

## Current evidence

- Working browser-local CSV audit.
- Tested two-signal rule, reason codes, allowlist, and export.
- Hosted BuyerOrigin product surface.
- Architecture and privacy boundaries documented.

## Missing evidence

- External merchants.
- Paid usage.
- Measured false-positive rate on real data.
- Live Shopify integration.
- Demonstrated retention or repeat workflow use.

## Why Quillgeist moved back to #1

The product now has a stronger evidence loop, not just a broader feature list.

Quillgeist can separate model judgment from deterministic execution, measure aggregate execution/compaction activity, and publish an ongoing progress journal that distinguishes measured results from estimates and internal proof from external traction.

The product shape is also clearer:

`local runtime -> MCP/API interoperability -> web portal SaaS`

The local runtime creates control and execution value. MCP/API makes that value portable across models. The portal can become the recurring paid relationship for synchronization, usage/savings analytics, workflow reuse, policy, team governance, and billing.

That does **not** mean external traction has been proven. The next evidence gate is still outside repeat users and paid behavior.

## Current YC posture

Quillgeist is the current likely Winter 2027 application path.

The public progress journal should be used as an evidence surface, not as a marketing substitute for user traction. Internal role-specific CRM builds can demonstrate technical efficiency and verification. They must remain labeled internal until outside users reproduce the behavior.

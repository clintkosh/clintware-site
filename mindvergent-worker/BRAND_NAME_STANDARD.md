# MindVergent™ brand naming standard

Public brand name: **MindVergent™**
Community/product name: **MindVergent™ Labs**

Use the capital V in every public-facing reference. Use the ™ immediately after MindVergent in public brand display and prose references where visually reasonable. Do not write Mindvergent, MINDVERGENT, or MindVergent without ™ as the brand name.

Technical identifiers remain lowercase where required by DNS, URLs, file paths, worker names, and infrastructure, including `mindvergent.com`, `mindvergent.clintware.com`, and `mindvergent-worker`.

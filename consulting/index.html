FAQ

Questions before the diagnostic.

Clintware engagements are designed around operational evidence and clear boundaries, not open-ended consulting hours.

Do you replace our support or Customer Success team?

No. Clintware can temporarily supplement capacity, but the main objective is to improve the operating model, enable the existing team, and define what should remain human, be reassigned, or be automated.

Can you help before we hire a full-time leader?

Yes. Fractional work can establish the process, reporting, ownership, and operating cadence before the company commits to a permanent role.

Is this only for cybersecurity companies?

No. Clintware supports technical B2B SaaS broadly, with specialized experience in cybersecurity, email security, phishing protection, technical onboarding, and customer-risk communication.

Will AI eliminate our need for human support?

That is not the premise. AI is most useful for stable repetitive work, retrieval, classification, summarization, suggested responses, and routing. Human judgment remains essential for ambiguity, trust, security, escalation, and customer outcomes.

Can you implement the recommendations?

Yes. Clintware can design the process, build the operating materials, support rollout, train the team, provide temporary execution capacity, and help measure whether the change is working.

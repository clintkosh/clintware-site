Quillgeist Terms

Last updated: September 24, 2026
The Clintware-hosted Quillgeist domain is a software/documentation distribution surface. Public runtime, account, pairing, telemetry, REST-execution, and MCP-execution routes on that domain are retired. Optional cloud functionality is self-hosted.

1. Software

Quillgeist provides local-first AI operating-context, execution, verification, compaction, and related developer functionality. Public builds start local-only.

2. Self-hosting

If you deploy Quillgeist Cloud, you are responsible for that deployment, its infrastructure, credentials, access controls, logs, retention, availability, and compliance. Do not use Clintware credentials or endpoints as your runtime.

3. Local execution

Execution features can change files, run processes, or interact with repositories when authorized by local policy. Review permissions, backups, and generated changes appropriate to the task.

4. Security

Do not use Quillgeist to access systems without authorization, bypass safeguards, or expose unrestricted administrative execution to the internet. Keep provider credentials in stores you control.

5. Estimates

Token counts, savings, cost projections, and similar estimated measurements are approximations, not provider billing records or guarantees.

6. Availability

Clintware may update or retire distribution-site functionality as needed. Self-hosted availability is controlled by the operator of that deployment.

7. Intellectual property

Quillgeist and Clintware software, branding, documentation, and service design remain the property of their respective owners, subject to applicable licenses. Users retain rights they otherwise hold in their own content.

8. Privacy

See the Quillgeist Privacy policy.

9. Contact

Questions about the Clintware distribution site can be directed through clintware.com.

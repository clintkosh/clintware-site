Product shape: portal + local runtime + MCP/API

The web portal is the human-facing SaaS surface. The local runtime remains the execution authority. MCP/API is the machine interface connecting models, approved capabilities, and the portal/control plane.

  • Quillgeist local runtime: permissions, deterministic execution, verification, private state, and local-first operation.
  • Quillgeist web portal: progress, personal usage/savings, workflows, policy management, team controls, billing, and future cross-device sync.
  • MCP/API: model-agnostic capability protocol and orchestration interface, not the entire customer experience by itself.

Evidence boundary

  • Measured: counts, durations, pass/fail status, files changed, and browser checks emitted by instrumentation.
  • Estimated: token fields and remote-only counterfactual savings unless a provider reports exact billing tokens for both sides of a controlled comparison.
  • Internal proof: founder/Clintware production use. Useful product evidence, but not external-customer traction.
  • External traction: outside repeat users, paid users, retention, and revenue will only appear when verified.

# Clintware Family Media Studio — make-it-real checklist

## Recommended public promise

**Picture yourself anywhere. Become almost anything.**

Send photos you have permission to use and describe the moment you want: a Manhattan rooftop, a storybook kingdom, a futuristic command deck, a vintage portrait studio, or something entirely your own. Clintware turns the idea into polished AI-assisted artwork, with a real person reviewing every order.

Supporting line:

**Ask for the idea you actually want. If an exact celebrity, logo, character, uniform, or branded location cannot be used, Clintware will offer the closest original alternative with the same energy.**

Do not use “be anyone.” It can imply impersonating another real person. Use:

- Become almost anything.
- Step into almost any role.
- Picture yourself almost anywhere.
- Bring us the impossible moment.
- Keep the vision. Make the details original.

## Launch sequence

### 1. Set the operating identity

- Confirm the public seller name: Clintware Family Media Studio.
- Create a working `studio@clintware.com` address or forwarder.
- File the applicable assumed-name/DBA paperwork while operating as a sole proprietor.
- Start a separate business ledger and preferably a separate checking account.
- Apply for the Texas Sales and Use Tax Permit before accepting taxable paid orders.
- Reserve sales tax, estimated federal tax, refunds, processing fees, and generation costs separately from profit.

### 2. Select and document the actual fulfillment tools

For the first orders, use a manual workflow rather than an automated customer-facing API.

- Choose the image-generation/editing service.
- Confirm its current commercial-use terms.
- Confirm whether customer uploads may be used for model improvement and disable that use where possible.
- Confirm its retention/deletion controls.
- Record the provider in the Privacy Policy before launch.
- Keep human review for every real-person likeness and every image containing a minor.

### 3. Finish the website configuration

- Upload `family-media` to `public_html` on PHP-capable Namecheap hosting.
- Set `STUDIO_EMAIL` in `config.php`.
- Verify SSL and HTTPS.
- Confirm private uploads are stored outside `public_html`.
- Remove every developer or launch-placeholder warning visible to customers.
- Replace all payment placeholders with live PayPal invoices or PayPal-hosted Payment Links only after taxes and policies are configured.
- Add a main-site navigation link to `/family-media/`.

### 4. Configure the products and checkout

- Free Portrait Preview: one watermarked concept, no commercial use, no revision.
- Portrait Mini: $29, two final images, one revision.
- Studio Session: $79, six final images, two revisions.
- Brand & Creator Session: $179, twelve final images, three revisions, commercial-use license.
- Collect name, email, billing address, and terms acceptance in checkout.
- Use the same email to match checkout and order submission.
- State when production begins, because that changes refund eligibility.

### 5. Build a real sample portfolio

Create at least 9 high-quality samples before promotion:

1. Manhattan rooftop family scene
2. Maroon-and-white game-day scene
3. Storybook family scene
4. Futuristic command-deck portrait
5. Original stadium performer scene
6. Original city guardian poster
7. Holiday card
8. Coloring-page conversion
9. Calendar collection

Use invented adults or properly released models. Do not use customer work publicly without a separate marketing release. Show the source-to-result process only when permission covers both images.

### 6. Test the order process

- Submit a free test order.
- Submit a PayPal invoice test using an email you control.
- Confirm email notification.
- Confirm source files are inaccessible from the public web.
- Confirm order references and consent timestamps are saved.
- Complete one full proof/revision/final-delivery cycle.
- Test the deletion procedure and document the result.
- Test a declined request and an original-alternative offer.

### 7. Fulfill each first-run order manually

1. Verify payment and email match.
2. Review uploader consent and minor consent.
3. Inspect images for quality and prohibited sensitive content.
4. Translate restricted details into original alternatives.
5. Send a written concept summary before expensive generation when the request is ambiguous.
6. Create a low-resolution proof.
7. Apply only the included revision rounds.
8. Export final JPG/PNG files and license terms.
9. Send a delivery message identifying AI assistance and any important limitations.
10. Record revenue, tax, fees, generation cost, labor time, refund reserve, and net profit.
11. Delete source and working files on the disclosed schedule.

### 8. Promote carefully

Lead with the customer outcome, not the AI tool:

- Strong: “Put your family inside the scene you have always imagined.”
- Weak: “We use cutting-edge generative AI.”

Post genuine samples, transparent prices, turnaround, revision limits, and the person behind the service. On Reddit, participate before promoting, disclose the business connection, follow each community’s rules, and use designated promotion or feedback threads.

### 9. Automate only after demand is proven

Automation trigger: at least 10 paid orders with a documented successful workflow.

Then add, server-side only:

- PayPal webhook verification
- Private object storage
- Upload scanning and moderation
- Provider API call
- Low-resolution proofs
- Customer approval portal
- Expiring download links
- Scheduled file deletion
- Error logging and manual-review queue

Never place an API secret in browser JavaScript.

### 10. Form the LLC without draining the business

Use the existing milestone: $500 cumulative net profit or 10 paid orders, whichever comes first, while leaving tax and refund reserves untouched.

## Request wording examples

### Location

Instead of: “a generic building in New York”

Use: “a Manhattan rooftop beneath an original art-deco skyline at blue hour, with warm office lights, street reflections, and no recognizable corporate signs.”

### Music-performance fantasy

Instead of: “me performing with Taylor Swift”

Use: “me as the headliner of an original stadium-pop concert, wearing custom sequined stagewear, surrounded by dramatic spotlights, friendship bracelets, and a cheering crowd.”

### College game day

Instead of: “my family at Texas A&M with Reveille”

Use: “my family on a maroon-and-white college game-day field, with custom fan signs, a festive tailgate atmosphere, and an original collie companion without official marks.”

### Superhero fantasy

Instead of: “make me Batman”

Use: “make me an original nocturnal city guardian in a custom armored suit, with a unique emblem, dramatic cape-like silhouette, and an art-deco city at night.”

### Famous landmark fantasy

Instead of: “me hanging a flag from the Empire State Building”

Use: “me on the rooftop of a towering Manhattan art-deco skyscraper, safely raising a custom flag above an original skyline in a cinematic adventure scene.”

## Order-form prompt formula

Ask customers to complete:

- **Who:** people or pets shown, plus required likeness details
- **Role:** what they become or are doing
- **Place:** city, environment, or original world
- **Era:** modern, historical, retro, futuristic, timeless
- **Wardrobe:** colors, materials, level of formality
- **Mood:** joyful, dramatic, mysterious, playful, elegant
- **Camera:** close portrait, full-body, wide cinematic, overhead, poster
- **Format:** square, portrait, landscape, print, social, card, calendar
- **Must keep:** faces, hair, glasses, pet markings, jewelry, body proportions
- **Avoid:** unwanted colors, text, props, themes, or facial changes

## Copy rule

Promise range, not unlimited execution:

**Nearly any original scene you can describe, subject to consent, safety, technical feasibility, and third-party rights.**

Do not publish:

- Anything you can imagine, guaranteed.
- Be anyone you want.
- Exact celebrity likenesses.
- Officially licensed-looking brand scenes without a license.
- Guaranteed perfect facial likeness.
- Exclusive copyright guaranteed.

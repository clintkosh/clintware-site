# Ridge Director of Customer Experience application

Public role-specific application microsite for Clinton Kosh.

Canonical URL: https://ridge.clintware.com

## Design intent

- Evidence-first, editorial, industrial treatment influenced by Ridge's current product presentation without copying logos, proprietary assets, or site text.
- Public-safe contact: clint@clintware.com only.
- No personal phone, Gmail, street address, recruiter content, compensation details, or private interview material.
- DTC concepts are clearly presented as proposed operating approaches rather than prior ownership claims.

## QA

Deployment workflow validates:
- semantic structure and required content;
- private-contact exclusions;
- public resume privacy;
- PDF metadata/text sanity;
- custom-domain deployment;
- HTTPS, headers, health endpoint and first-party links.

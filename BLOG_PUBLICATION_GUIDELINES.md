# Clintware blog publication standard

Every Clintware blog post must use basic, public-safe information.

- Do not name outside companies, customers, prospective employers, individual contacts, or private projects.
- Do not disclose job-pipeline details, applications, interview stages, compensation discussions, or non-public work.
- Use only high-level, aggregate career statistics that have been explicitly cleared for public use.
- Describe credentials by subject area unless naming a provider has been explicitly approved for that post.
- Do not imply a relationship, endorsement, deployment, or client result that is not public and verifiable.
- Keep examples anonymized and focused on the method, lesson, or capability.

This is the default for all future Clintware blog posts.  It may be changed only by an explicit, post-specific instruction from the site owner.

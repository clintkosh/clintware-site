Why this system maps to the role

The prototype is intentionally shaped around the published success metrics and responsibilities.

Manage the mentor team

Mentor scorecards, 1:1 cadence, coaching notes, 30-day improvement plans, and escalations.

Own fellow delivery

Exception-based fellow risk, milestone roll-up, PR review SLA, and intervention ownership.

Replace hand-tracking

One stateful delivery surface instead of parallel spreadsheets and status chasing.

Close curriculum loop

Cross-pod pattern clustering with evidence-backed curriculum change requests and shipped-state tracking.

Hold quality

Behavior-based mentor rubric and concrete development plans rather than vague coaching.

Design basis

Official role requirements first; AI patterns second; management practice as a supporting layer.
